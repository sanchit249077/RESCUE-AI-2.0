# RescueAI real-data source plan

## Pilot dataset
Zenodo record 8169506:
https://zenodo.org/records/8169506

The published record describes a multi-temporal Southern Sikkim landslide inventory. It reports 440 mapped landslides from Google Earth imagery (2002–2019), with field ground-truthing during a 12-day visit in 2019, plus topographic and geology attributes.

## Official sources to add
- GSI Bhusanket: https://bhusanket.gsi.gov.in/
- ISRO Bhuvan: https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/
- IMD: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/

The first inventory is for susceptibility/data-engineering development. It is not sufficient by itself for a live early-warning model; rainfall and other trigger observations must be time-aligned to landslide occurrence.
